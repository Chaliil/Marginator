Marginator

by Khalil Kiser

This small tool lets you add a white box to your PDF files to take notes in. It lets you take notes on the same page in an app like Goodnotes if you are working with slide decks or documents. It even removes encryption from PDFs as long as you have the correct password.

Download for macOS
